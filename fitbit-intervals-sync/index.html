fitbit-intervals-sync

A personal tool that copies the author's own Fitbit wellness data (sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, SpO2, respiratory rate, steps, weight, body fat and VO2 max) from the Google Health API into the author's own intervals.icu account once a day.

It is used by one person, its author, and is not offered to the public.
